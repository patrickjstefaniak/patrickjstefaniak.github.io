Patrick Stefaniak is a Indiana based artist who works in crochet, videogames, 3D printing, drawing/painting, animation, and performance to think about labor, rendering, craft, perception, and queerness. He is currently a Visiting Assistant Professor in Digital Art at the Eskenazi School of Art, Architecture + Design at Indiana University, Bloomington and recieved an MFA in Digital Arts + New Media at UC Santa Cruz in 2021.
